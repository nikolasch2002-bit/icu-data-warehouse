CI fails if this file no longer matches the queries.

## 01 Cohort counts

How many patients, hospital admissions and ICU stays the warehouse holds, overall and broken down by anchor year group and by the care unit where each ICU stay began. Every later result should be read against these numbers: with 100 patients, a single death moves a unit's mortality by several percentage points.

The anchor year group is the real 3-year window a patient's shifted dates map to; it is the only real-calendar time MIMIC reveals.

An admission whose ICU stays began in two different units counts once in each, so the care-unit rows add up to more than the total admissions.
